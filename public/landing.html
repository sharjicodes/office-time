Simple from the first tap

Your day, step by step

No complicated setup. Open Milo when you start, keep your sessions up to date, and review how the day went.

01

Choose how to begin

Sign in with your email to keep your account data in sync across devices, or continue in local mode and keep attendance on this device. You can also join chat as a guest with a shareable username.

02

Punch in and work

Tap Punch in when you begin a work session. Milo’s net-work timer counts only time you’re punched in. Punch out for a break; Milo tracks the break until you punch in again.

03

Finish and look back

Use Office out to finish the day and see a work summary. History keeps daily sessions and totals, with tools to correct a punch or export a month’s report.

Good to know

Before you get started

A few useful details about accounts, syncing, and reminders.

Can I use Milo without creating an account?

Yes. Continue in offline mode to use attendance on that device. Local attendance does not automatically appear on your other devices. Sign in when you want to use your account’s cloud features and cross-device sync.

What is needed for chats, calls, and online games?

Those features need an internet connection and the shared Milo services. Use usernames to find people or invite them. Ludo also has an offline option.

Will every reminder arrive when Milo is closed?

Closed-browser reminders require background push to be configured, enabled, and permitted on a supported browser/device. iPhone and iPad web push has specific installation and browser requirements. Device notification settings can also affect delivery and sound.

Can Milo replace my employer’s official attendance system?

Use the attendance process approved by your employer. Milo is a companion tracker unless your HR team has authorized it for official attendance.
